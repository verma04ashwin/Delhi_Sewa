# DEC-0002 — Discovery First

**Date:** 2026-08-13

**Status:** Accepted

**Area:** Product

## Context

DelhiSewa is an early-stage product and the core problem has not yet been validated at scale.

## Decision

The initial product will focus primarily on local discovery rather than attempting to build a complete marketplace.

## Reasoning

Discovery is the simplest way to test whether DelhiSewa provides value to residents and businesses.

Marketplace capabilities introduce substantially more complexity.

## Deferred

- Payments
- Delivery
- Complex booking
- Full transactions
- Advanced marketplace functionality

## Revisit When

Real usage and research demonstrate that users need transactional capabilities.
