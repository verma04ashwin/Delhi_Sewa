# Geographic Strategy

## Initial Geography

**Jwala Nagar, Shahdara, Delhi 110032**

## Why Start Small?

The initial geographic scope should remain small enough to:

- Understand local users
- Personally interact with businesses
- Maintain high data quality
- Build local density
- Observe real behavior
- Iterate quickly

## Expansion Principle

Geographic expansion should happen only when the current area demonstrates sufficient product usefulness.

## Potential Expansion Path

Jwala Nagar
→ Nearby localities
→ Shahdara
→ Larger parts of Delhi
→ NCR

This is a hypothesis, not a fixed roadmap.

## Expansion Criteria

To be defined through actual learning.

Potential criteria may include:

- User engagement
- Search volume
- Listing coverage
- Business participation
- Repeat usage
- Successful discovery
- Operational capacity
