# Target Businesses

## Initial Business Types

DelhiSewa may include:

- Retail shops
- Local service providers
- Professionals
- Clinics
- Restaurants and food businesses
- Repair services
- Home services
- Educational services
- Local institutions
- Other useful places

## Business Value Proposition

The initial value proposition is local digital discoverability.

Potential future value may include:

- Leads
- Enquiries
- Reputation
- Customer engagement
- Offers
- Booking
- Transactions

These future capabilities require validation.

## Research Questions

- What problems do local businesses have with digital discovery?
- How do they currently acquire customers?
- What information can they maintain?
- What would make DelhiSewa useful to them?
- What would make them actively participate?
