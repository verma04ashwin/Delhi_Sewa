# Problem Statement

## Working Problem

Local residents often need to find nearby businesses, shops, professionals, services, and useful places but local information can be fragmented across search engines, maps, social networks, messaging groups, word of mouth, and offline sources.

DelhiSewa is exploring whether a focused hyperlocal platform can make this discovery process simpler and more useful.

## Important

This is currently a hypothesis.

It must be validated through research and actual usage.

## Questions

- How do residents currently discover local businesses?
- What information do they need?
- What makes a local listing trustworthy?
- What discovery problems are poorly served today?
- Which categories have the strongest demand?
