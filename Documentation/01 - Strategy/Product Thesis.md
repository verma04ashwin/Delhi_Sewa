# Product Thesis

## Initial Thesis

A focused, high-quality, hyperlocal directory can provide value if it makes local businesses and services easier to discover than fragmented existing sources.

## Initial Approach

Start with:

- One locality
- Useful categories
- High-quality listings
- Clear business information
- Simple discovery
- Simple search
- Real-world validation

## What We Are Deliberately Avoiding

- Premature marketplace complexity
- Large geographic expansion
- Unvalidated feature development
- Complex transactions
- Features that do not directly improve discovery

## Validation

This thesis should be continuously tested through research, experiments, and usage data.
