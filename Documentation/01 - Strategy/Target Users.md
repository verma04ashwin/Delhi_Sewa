# Target Users

## Primary User

Local residents looking for nearby:

- Businesses
- Shops
- Services
- Professionals
- Useful places

## Current Hypothesis

The most valuable users may be residents who frequently need local services or businesses and currently rely on fragmented discovery methods.

## Validation Required

This definition should be refined through actual user research.

## Research Questions

- Who uses local discovery most frequently?
- What do they search for?
- How do they currently find providers?
- What information influences their choice?
- What makes them trust a listing?
- What would make them return to DelhiSewa?
