# Vision

## Vision

Make it easy for people to discover useful local businesses, services, professionals, shops, and places in their locality.

DelhiSewa should eventually become a trusted local discovery and marketplace layer connecting residents with the businesses and services around them.

## Long-Term Direction

The initial product is intentionally narrow.

The long-term product may expand from discovery into additional capabilities when there is sufficient evidence that users and businesses need them.

Potential future capabilities may include:

- Business claiming
- Reviews and reputation
- Enquiries
- Booking
- Offers
- Lead generation
- Transactions
- Other marketplace functionality

These are possibilities, not current commitments.

## Guiding Principle

Expand based on evidence, not assumptions.
