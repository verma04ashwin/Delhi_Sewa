# DelhiSewa

> **DelhiSewa is a hyperlocal marketplace and local discovery platform connecting people with nearby services, shops, professionals, and businesses.**


---
## 1. Project Overview

DelhiSewa is being built as a **hyperlocal marketplace** focused on helping people discover and interact with local businesses and service providers.

The platform is intended to bring together multiple forms of local commerce and services into one ecosystem.

At a high level, DelhiSewa connects:

```text
                    DELHISEWA
                        │
          ┌─────────────┴─────────────┐
          │                           │
       CUSTOMERS                  LOCAL SUPPLY
          │                           │
   Residents / Users       ┌──────────┼──────────┐
                           │          │          │
                         Shops   Professionals  Services
                           │          │          │
                           └──────────┼──────────┘
                                      │
                               Local Businesses
```

The initial focus is not simply on building an application.

The larger objective is to build a **local marketplace ecosystem** in which customers can discover relevant local options and businesses can acquire customers, build their presence, and eventually transact through DelhiSewa.

---

# 2. Vision

### Vision

Build a trusted and useful digital layer for local commerce and services, making it easier for people to discover and engage with businesses around them.

DelhiSewa aims to make local discovery:

- easier
    
- more relevant
    
- more trustworthy
    
- more accessible
    
- more connected to actual local commerce
    

The long-term vision is to become a platform where a person can answer:

> **"What do I need nearby, who provides it, and how can I contact or use them?"**

through one ecosystem.

---

# 3. The Problem

Local businesses and service providers often exist within a fragmented ecosystem.

A customer may need to search across:

- Google
    
- social media
    
- messaging apps
    
- local directories
    
- recommendations from friends
    
- physical signage
    
- classified platforms
    
- individual business websites
    

At the same time, local businesses and professionals may struggle with:

- online visibility
    
- customer acquisition
    
- discovering customer demand
    
- managing leads
    
- building trust
    
- maintaining accurate business information
    
- competing for attention online
    

DelhiSewa intends to investigate whether a focused hyperlocal marketplace can solve meaningful parts of these problems.

# 5. The Marketplace

DelhiSewa should be thought of as a **two-sided marketplace**.

There are two primary sides:

## Demand

People who need something.

Examples:

- "I need a plumber."
    
- "Where can I find a nearby stationery shop?"
    
- "Which tutor is available?"
    
- "Where can I get my phone repaired?"
    
- "Which local businesses provide this service?"
    

## Supply

People and organizations that provide something.

Examples:

- local shops
    
- professionals
    
- service providers
    
- businesses
    
- independent operators
    

The fundamental marketplace loop is:

```text
Customer has a need
        ↓
Customer searches/discovers
        ↓
DelhiSewa presents relevant local options
        ↓
Customer evaluates options
        ↓
Customer contacts / enquires / books
        ↓
Provider receives customer
        ↓
Service / transaction occurs
        ↓
Customer provides feedback
        ↓
DelhiSewa becomes more useful
```

This marketplace loop is one of the core systems that the project must validate.

# 11. Geographic Strategy

DelhiSewa is intended to be **hyperlocal**.

The initial strategy should prioritize depth over breadth.

Instead of attempting to immediately cover a very large geographical area, the project should investigate whether DelhiSewa can achieve strong marketplace liquidity within specific local areas.

The expansion model may eventually resemble:

```text
Locality
   ↓
Neighborhood
   ↓
District / Area
   ↓
City
   ↓
New City
```

The exact geographic rollout strategy remains to be validated.

# 12. Core Marketplace Principles

The project should be guided by several principles.

### Local First

The platform should prioritize relevance to the user's local context.

### Trust

Accurate information, verification, reviews, and transparent marketplace behavior are important.

### Utility Before Monetization

The platform must first create meaningful value for users and providers before optimizing monetization.

### Evidence Over Assumptions

Important decisions should be supported by research, experiments, or measurable evidence.

### Marketplace Balance

DelhiSewa must create value for both sides:

```text
Customer Value
       ↕
DelhiSewa
       ↕
Provider / Business Value
```

### Sustainable Economics

Growth without healthy unit economics is not the ultimate goal.

DelhiSewa's ultimate success is not defined by the number of features built.

Success means creating a marketplace where:

```text
Customers
    ↓
Can reliably find useful local options
    ↓
Providers
    ↓
Receive meaningful demand
    ↓
Businesses
    ↓
Gain measurable value
    ↓
DelhiSewa
    ↓
```

- real customer demand
    
- real provider participation
    
- repeated usage
    
- successful customer-provider connections
    
- strong trust
    
- measurable marketplace liquidity
