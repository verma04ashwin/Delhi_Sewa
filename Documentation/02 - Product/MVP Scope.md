# MVP Scope

## Objective

Build the smallest useful version of DelhiSewa that can test whether local residents find value in organized local discovery.

## In Scope

- Local business discovery
- Categories
- Search
- Business/service listings
- Basic business information
- Location information
- Contact information
- Useful places

## Potentially In Scope

To be evaluated during product definition:

- Filtering
- Maps
- Business verification
- Business claiming

## Out of Scope for Initial MVP

Unless research changes the decision:

- Payments
- Delivery
- Complex booking
- Full marketplace transactions
- Advanced social features
- Large-scale geographic expansion

## Success Criteria

To be defined before launch.

Success should be measured through real user behavior rather than only feature completion.
