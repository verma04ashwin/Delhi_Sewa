# Product Roadmap

## Now

Current MVP and validation work.

- Local discovery
- Categories
- Search
- Business/service listings
- Basic business information
- Locality-focused experience

## Next

Potential improvements based on early validation.

- Better filtering
- Verification
- Business claiming
- Improved discovery
- Saved businesses
- Better business profiles

## Later

Potential marketplace capabilities.

- Reviews
- Enquiries
- Booking
- Offers
- Lead generation
- Transactions

## Not Now

Features intentionally deferred because they add complexity before the core discovery problem is validated.

- Payments
- Delivery
- Full ecommerce
- Large-scale social features

## Principle

The roadmap is evidence-driven and may change.
