# Product Overview

## Product

DelhiSewa is a hyperlocal discovery platform.

## Initial Purpose

Help residents discover local:

- Businesses
- Shops
- Services
- Professionals
- Useful places

## Initial Geography

Jwala Nagar, Shahdara, Delhi 110032

## Product Philosophy

The first version should be:

- Simple
- Useful
- Fast to understand
- Locally relevant
- Data-focused
- Easy to maintain

## Current Product Boundary

DelhiSewa is initially a discovery platform.

Marketplace, transactions, booking, payments, and other capabilities should only be introduced when supported by evidence.
