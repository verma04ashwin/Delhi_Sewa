# DelhiSewa

> **A hyperlocal local-business discovery platform, starting with Jwala Nagar and Shahdara, Delhi.**

## 1. What is DelhiSewa?

DelhiSewa is an early-stage hyperlocal platform being built initially for **Jwala Nagar and the surrounding Shahdara area of Delhi**.

The first version is intentionally simple.

The goal is to help local residents **discover businesses, shops, services, professionals, and other useful places in their locality** through one organized digital platform.

In its initial form, DelhiSewa is similar in concept to a **hyperlocal Justdial-style directory and discovery platform**, but with the intention of gradually developing into a more capable local marketplace.

The project will start small, learn from real users and local businesses, and expand only when there is evidence that the product is useful.

---

# 2. Initial Geographic Scope

## Phase 1 — Jwala Nagar / Shahdara

DelhiSewa will initially focus on Jwala Nagar and work as a local business repository.

The initial objective is **not to cover all of Delhi**.
Instead, the goal is to build a sufficiently useful local directory within a limited geographic area.


### Why start locally?

A hyperlocal product needs local density.

Rather than trying to list thousands of businesses across Delhi with little depth, DelhiSewa will initially attempt to build a useful and accurate collection of businesses within a small geographic area.

```text
                    DELHISEWA
                        │
                        ▼
              Jwala Nagar / Shahdara
                        │
          ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
          ▼             ▼             ▼
       Shops         Services      Professionals
          │             │             │
          └─────────────┼─────────────┘
                        ▼
                  Local Discovery
```

If the model works locally, the geographic footprint can gradually expand.

# 3. Initial Product

The first version of DelhiSewa is primarily a **local business directory and discovery platform**.

Users should be able to find relevant local businesses and services based on things such as:

- category
    
- business name
    
- locality
    
- location
    
- service
    
- keywords
    
- potentially other useful filters
    

Examples:

> Find electricians near Jwala Nagar.

> Find nearby medical shops.

> Find local restaurants.

> Find mobile repair shops.

> Find tuition centers.

> Find plumbers.

> Find local clothing stores.

> Find professionals and service providers.

The initial product should prioritize **discovery and useful information** over complex marketplace functionality.

# 6. What DelhiSewa Is NOT Yet

At the current stage, DelhiSewa is **not yet** intended to be:

- a full-service marketplace
    
- an e-commerce platform
    
- a delivery platform
    
- a large booking platform
    
- a payment platform
    
- a city-wide platform
    
- a nationwide platform
    
- a comprehensive super app
    

These may become future directions, but they are not part of the initial product definition.

The project should not build complexity before there is evidence that users need it.

# 8. Business Model

The business model is currently **undecided**.

At the beginning, the priority is to determine:

1. Do local users find DelhiSewa useful?
    
2. Will local businesses participate?
    
3. Which business categories have the most demand?
    
4. What information do users actually need?
    
5. How frequently will users return?
    
6. What value does DelhiSewa create for businesses?
    
7. Which business model can eventually capture part of that value?
    

Potential future monetization models may include:

- paid business listings
    
- promoted listings
    
- advertising
    
- lead generation
    
- business subscriptions
    
- premium business tools
    
- commissions
    
- booking fees
    
- other marketplace models
    

No monetization model should be treated as final until it has been validated.

# 10. Product Principles

### Start Small

Build for one locality before attempting to build for an entire city.

### Local Density

A small area with useful, accurate information is more valuable than a huge area with incomplete information.

### Simplicity

The initial product should solve one problem well.

### Accuracy

Business information should be as accurate and useful as possible.

### Discovery First

The initial product focuses primarily on helping people find local businesses and services.

### Evidence Before Expansion

Features and geographic expansion should be driven by actual usage and research.

### Don't Overbuild

Do not build complex marketplace functionality until there is a demonstrated need for it.

# 11. Initial Marketplace Model

At the beginning, DelhiSewa is primarily a **local discovery platform** rather than a complex two-sided transactional marketplace.

The initial relationship is:

```text
                  DELHISEWA
                      │
          ┌───────────┴───────────┐
          ▼                       ▼
       LOCAL USERS          LOCAL BUSINESSES
          │                       │
          │                       │
          └────── Discovery ──────┘
```

DelhiSewa provides the discovery layer.

The customer may then:

- call the business
    
- visit the business
    
- contact the business
    
- make an enquiry
    

Later, DelhiSewa may facilitate more of the transaction itself if there is a strong reason to do so.

# 13. The Core Question

The initial question DelhiSewa is trying to answer is:

> **Can a focused, accurate, and useful digital directory make it easier for people in Jwala Nagar and Shahdara to discover local businesses and services?**

> **What additional value can DelhiSewa provide to both local customers and local businesses?**

Only after answering these questions should the platform expand into a more sophisticated marketplace.

# 17. Definition of Success for Phase 1

Phase 1 will be successful if DelhiSewa can build a genuinely useful local directory for Jwala Nagar and Shahdara.

Success should be measured through real-world behavior rather than feature count.

Important signals include:

- useful local listings
    
- accurate business information
    
- users searching for businesses
    
- users discovering businesses
    
- users contacting businesses
    
- businesses wanting to be listed
    
- businesses returning to the platform
    
- repeat users
    
- organic/local growth
    

The immediate objective is not to become the biggest platform.

The objective is to become **useful in one locality first**.

## Final Principle

> **Start with Jwala Nagar. Become useful in Shahdara. Earn the right to expand.**
